Know Your Waste Categories

Proper separation at the household level improves recycling efficiency.

Wet Waste

Food scraps, fruit and vegetable peels. Ideal for local composting.

Dry Waste

Clean plastics, cardboard, newspapers, and packaging materials.

E-Waste

Batteries, cables, broken appliances, and electronic parts.

Hazardous Waste

Paint cans, medical waste, and chemicals requiring cautious handling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the complaint process and platform operations.

Click on the File Complaint button, select your district/location, provide street landmark details, attach a photo of the waste spot, and click Register.

Navigate to Track Complaints from the menu bar to view recorded tickets and see whether the status is Marked as Pending, In Progress, or Completed.

Assigned Municipal Officers and local sanitation teams review ticket details, dispatch cleaning staff to the marked location, and mark tickets resolved.
